# Beel Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 19, 2026

**Publisher:** Power Ranjer

**Contact:** [robby396fs@gmail.com](mailto:robby396fs@gmail.com)

Beel helps you record billiards gameplay, create highlights, and organize and share video clips. This policy explains how Beel handles your information.

## Information processed on your device

Beel processes gameplay videos and creates highlights directly on your device using models included with the app. Your recordings, audio, imported videos, clips, and analysis results are not uploaded to us or to an external AI service.

Beel stores clips, session details, albums, favorites, trim settings, cue annotations, analysis results, and preferences locally to provide its features. It also checks available storage and uses device timing to manage recording and processing.

Beel does not require an account, use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, track you across apps or websites, or sell your personal information.

## Camera, microphone, and Photos

**Camera:** With your permission, Beel uses the camera to record gameplay.

**Microphone:** With your permission, recordings and generated clips include audio, which may contain table sounds and nearby voices. You can record video without microphone access.

**Selected videos:** Beel uses the system Photos picker to access videos you choose for importing and analysis. If a selected video is stored in iCloud Photos, Apple may download it to your device.

**Saving recordings:** With permission to add to Photos, Beel automatically attempts to save completed full recordings to your Photos library. These copies may sync through iCloud Photos according to your settings.

You can change camera, microphone, and Photos permissions in your device’s Settings.

## Sharing and backups

Beel shares a video when you choose a destination through the system share sheet. The receiving app, service, or person controls that copy.

Trimming changes playback within Beel. **Share Source Video** shares the complete underlying video file for that clip, including footage outside the selected trim range. Shared files may contain embedded metadata.

Your device backups may include Beel’s clip library and preferences, depending on your settings. Beel excludes its retained raw recording storage from device backups. Photos copies, exported files, and shared copies are managed separately.

Apple’s services are governed by [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Retention and deletion

Beel keeps clips and library information on your device until you delete them. Removing a clip from an album or deleting an album does not delete the clip from your library.

Temporary import copies are removed when no longer needed. Failed processing may retain a working copy so you can retry.

Beel may retain original or partial recordings and related review data when processing is interrupted, saving to Photos fails, or no highlights are generated. You can delete retained recordings under **Clips → Sessions → Recording Storage**.

Deleting the app removes its local app data from your device. Copies in Photos, exported files, shared destinations, and backups must be managed separately. Previously backed-up data may return if you restore a backup.

## Information you send us

If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you provide. We use this information to respond and retain it only as needed to resolve your request or meet applicable legal obligations.

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have sent us. We cannot remotely access or delete recordings and clips stored on your device.

## Security

Beel relies on iOS app storage protections and device security to protect locally stored information. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

## Children’s privacy

Beel is a general-audience billiards video tool. It does not require registration or knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. A parent or guardian may contact us about information a child has sent directly to us.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Beel’s features or privacy practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
