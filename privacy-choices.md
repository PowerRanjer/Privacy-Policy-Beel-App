# Beel User Privacy Choices

**Last updated:** November 19, 2026

**Publisher:** Power Ranjer

**Privacy contact:** [robby396fs@gmail.com](mailto:robby396fs@gmail.com)

Beel processes gameplay videos and creates highlights on your device. We do not receive your recordings, imported videos, generated clips, or library data through the app. This page explains how to control permissions, manage your content, and contact us about privacy.

## Manage camera and microphone permissions

Open **Settings → Apps → Beel** to change camera and microphone access. You can also manage these permissions under **Settings → Privacy & Security**.

- **Camera:** Turn off access to prevent Beel from recording new gameplay with the camera. You can still import a selected video and use existing clips.
- **Microphone:** Turn off access to record without microphone audio. Existing recordings retain any audio already captured.

Changing permissions affects future access. It does not delete recordings or clips already saved.

## Choose videos and control Photos saving

The system Photos picker lets you choose which video to import. Beel receives the selected video rather than broad access to your Photos library.

You can change Beel’s permission to add to Photos in iOS Settings. Turning off this permission prevents future automatic saves of completed full recordings to Photos. It does not prevent importing a video through the system picker or delete existing Photos copies.

If you use iCloud Photos, manage syncing and stored copies through Apple’s Photos and iCloud settings.

## Delete clips and retained recordings

- **Clips:** Delete clips from the Beel library to remove them. Removing a clip from an album or deleting an album keeps the clip in the library.
- **Retained recordings:** Go to **Clips → Sessions → Recording Storage** to delete retained original or partial recordings and their associated review data. Generated clips and Photos copies are managed separately.
- **All local app data:** Delete Beel from your device to remove its local app data. Offloading an app preserves its documents and data, so use Delete App when you intend to remove that data.
- **Other copies:** Manage videos saved to Photos, exported files, shared copies, and device backups in their respective destinations. Deleting a Beel copy does not remove those separate copies. Restoring a backup may restore previously saved app data.

## Control sharing

Beel sends a video to another app, service, or person when you choose a destination through the system share sheet.

**Share Source Video** shares the complete underlying video file for that clip, including footage outside the playback trim range. Review the video and the destination before sharing. To manage or delete a shared copy, use the receiving service’s controls or contact the recipient.

## Tracking, advertising, and data sales

Beel does not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, track you across other apps or websites, or sell your personal information. No opt-out setting is needed for these activities because Beel does not perform them.

## Request access, correction, or deletion

If you have sent information directly to Power Ranjer, such as an email or attachment, you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of that information by emailing [robby396fs@gmail.com](mailto:robby396fs@gmail.com) with the subject **Beel Privacy Request**.

Describe your request and identify the correspondence it concerns. Please send only the details needed to handle your request. We may ask for information needed to verify that the request concerns your information. We will respond in accordance with applicable privacy laws; some information may need to be retained to meet legal obligations.

We cannot remotely access or delete the recordings, clips, or library stored on your device. Use the in-app and device controls described above to manage that content.
